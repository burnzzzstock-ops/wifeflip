# Flipping Friend — Growth Plan (honest version for a very full season of life)

_Compiled July 2026. Fact-checked against current sources. Fees, tax rules, and platform
policies change — re-verify before leaning on a number._

Context that shapes everything below: this is run by one person — a stay-at-home mom of three
(oldest special needs, youngest a newborn) — part-time, on scraps of time. So this plan is sorted
by **how much of HER time it costs**, not by what a full-time reseller would do. Most "reselling
growth advice" assumes hours she doesn't have. Ignore that advice. Use this.

The single reframe: **the highest-leverage moves here cost her almost no time, and several are
things her husband can own entirely.** Growth right now = the app carrying the mental load +
whoever-has-a-spare-hour doing the few things that compound.

---

## Do these — they cost her almost zero time

### 1. Cross-list the inventory you ALREADY own (husband can do this in the evenings)
Same items, ~2–3× the buyers, **zero new sourcing**. Every item goes on eBay (primary) plus one or
two more by audience:
- Electronics, media, car parts, home goods → **Mercari** (flat 10% fee, easy catch-all)
- Mid-range clothing & handbags → **Poshmark** (20% fee but a higher-paying audience)
- Vintage tees / Y2K / streetwear → **Depop** (US fee now just **3.3% + $0.45**, no commission)
- Bulky / heavy / under-$15 → **Facebook Marketplace** local pickup (**0% fee**)

Do it by hand for a few weeks before paying for any tool. Expected effect: **20–40% more sales off
inventory you already have.** This is the biggest lever that adds no sourcing hours.

### 2. Set up the money side once (husband can fully own this)
This is the quiet compounder that turns "grocery money" into real income:
- **Free business checking** (Bluevine, Found, or a local credit union). Run all sourcing spend +
  payouts through it.
- **Write down a ~50% reinvestment rule.** Pay her a fixed draw; auto-reinvest ~half of net profit
  into inventory. Without a rule, profit quietly becomes groceries and the business never grows.
- **Capture the deductions** (shrinks the 15.3% self-employment tax): **mileage at 72.5¢/mile** for
  2026 (Goodwill, estate sales, post office, bank — 100 mi/week ≈ **$3,770/yr** deducted), plus the
  **home-office simplified method** ($5/sq ft, up to $1,500/yr, no receipts).
- **Tax bucket:** move 25–30% of net profit into a separate savings account as you earn. Quarterly
  estimate dates: **Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, 2026, and Jan 15, 2027.** (The app's tax export already
  gives your accountant the rest.)

### 3. Build a one-page "buy box" — one conversation, then it lives in her pocket
Her strengths: **heritage/vintage sportswear** (Champion, Carhartt, Levi's, Nike/Adidas) + shoes +
one hard-good category. Write down target brands, price floors, and instant-skip rules. Specialists
make the buy/skip call **in seconds** instead of researching each item, and turn inventory ~40%
faster. It also **solves the weak-signal store** — when she knows the brands cold, she doesn't need
a live price lookup in the aisle. (This should become an offline feature in the app — see below.)

### 4. Let the app do the tedious parts (already true, or nearly)
- **AI-written listings** — tap an item → *Generate eBay listing*. Cuts a 15–20 min listing to a
  1–3 min review.
- **The daily 2-minute routine** and the **"Do this next"** card mean she never has to plan or
  decide — she opens the app, does the one thing it shows her, and closes it.

## Explicitly SKIP these right now (they cost time she doesn't have)
Not because they're bad — because they're wrong for *this* season. Come back later.
- **Goodwill Outlet "bins" marathons** — high volume but hours of digging. Later.
- **Whatnot live selling** — a big show = 100+ packages to pack solo. Brutal right now.
- **Liquidation pallets** — capital, storage, and processing time she doesn't have. And never buy a
  pallet from a social-media ad.
- **Social media / building a following** — real but slow, and time-hungry. Not now.
- **Paid cross-listing tools, an LLC, a bigger eBay Store** — all lose money or time at this scale.
  An LLC only makes sense near ~$40k income; the free business bank account is what matters now.

---

## When she has more bandwidth (months/years out, not now)
The throughput-and-margin machine, in rough order: a ~$180 shipping station (thermal printer +
scale + free Pirate Ship) once shipping 40+/month; a SKU + numbered-bin system so nothing gets lost;
a weekly 30-min repricing ritual (staggered 5% Send-Offers-to-Watchers on aging listings); filling
**every** eBay item specific; driving toward free **eBay Top Rated Seller** status (10% fee
discount); a neighbor/friend **consignment pipeline** (inventory comes to her, ~$0 capital); and
handing the mechanical steps (photographing, packing, post-office runs) to husband/kids — the only
realistic "hiring" at this stage.

---

## The one number to watch
**Sell-through rate = sold ÷ (active + sold).** A seller at 65% on 200 listings beats one at 30% on
500. It compounds cash *and* feeds eBay's ranking. If it climbs as the listing count grows, the plan
is working. (Gut-check: profit per hour worked — so growth never becomes just more unpaid labor.)

---

## Biggest mistakes to avoid
- **Poshmark automation** (auto-share/relist/follow bots) — triggers suspensions. Sharing by hand is
  fine; bots are the line.
- **Chasing more store hours** instead of faster decisions (the buy box) and cross-listing.
- **Panic-discounting** — two staggered 5% cuts beat one 15% slash (each re-fires a watcher alert).
- **Drawing 100% of profit as grocery money** — with no reinvestment rule it's capped forever.
- **Assuming no 1099-K = no tax** — profit is taxable regardless (2026 threshold is back to $20k +
  200 sales, but the liability isn't).

---

## App features that would automate this growth (build queue)
1. **Offline "Buy Box"** — cached target brands, price floors, buy/skip rules that work with zero
   signal. Fixes the weak-signal store and powers specialization. _Highest impact._
2. **Per-platform net-profit-at-listing + cross-list tracker** — show take-home after each
   platform's exact 2026 fee, flag items live on fewer than 3 platforms, and prompt a "pull it
   everywhere" checklist when something sells (prevents double-selling).
3. **Aging queue + sell-through-by-category dashboard** — auto-flag 30/45/90-day listings into the
   day's offer/markdown to-do. (The aging alerts already exist; this extends them.)
4. **SKU + bin locator** — auto-SKU each item and show the bin number in the sold notification.
5. **Reinvest + tax dashboard** — live split of net profit into Pay Myself / Tax / Reinvest, a
   mileage logger, and quarterly-estimate reminders.
